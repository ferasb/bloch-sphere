Bloch Sphere

Apply gates to qubits and watch their states rotate.

An interactive 3D Bloch sphere for students meeting quantum computing for the first time. Apply the X, Y, Z, H, S and T gates, or a rotation by any angle, to up to five qubits; watch each gate turn the sphere; and see every state as a ket, a vector and probabilities. In the Gate Lab, combine gates into your own and see the single rotation they add up to.
