Privacy

Last updated 22 October 2026

This site uses no cookies, no analytics, no ads and no tracking of any kind. It doesn't load anything from other websites: its code and fonts are served from here.

What stays in your browser

To keep your work, the site saves it in your browser's own storage. None of it is ever sent anywhere.

  • Session storage (this tab only, gone when you close the tab): your journey, the Gate Lab's bench, and the challenge you're playing, so reloading the page doesn't lose them.
  • Local storage (this browser, until you delete it): the gates you save in My gates, the stars you earn in challenges, and your choice of light or dark theme, if you pick one.

This storage holds only what you build here and is used only to give it back to you. You can delete it at any time by clearing this site's data in your browser's settings.

Hosting

The site is hosted on GitHub Pages by GitHub, Inc. Like any web server, GitHub's receives your IP address when you open the page and may keep it in its logs for security. See GitHub's privacy statement.
